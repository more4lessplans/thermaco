Installation Details

What every install includes to Ontario code, and what's specific to your home.

Standard installation procedures

Every installation is completed in accordance with applicable Ontario code requirements and manufacturer specifications, including applicable TSSA and CSA B52 refrigeration requirements.

Refrigeration & Lineset

  • Deep vacuum evacuation — the system is evacuated to 500 microns or below, then the vacuum is held and verified using a digital micron gauge before the system is charged.
  • Nitrogen-purged lineset flush — the existing or new lineset is purged with nitrogen to help clear out debris, residual oils, and moisture before commissioning.
  • Silver-brazed refrigerant connections — all refrigerant joints are brazed under a continuous nitrogen purge to help prevent oxidation and contamination inside the refrigeration circuit.
  • Leak and pressure testing — the completed refrigeration circuit is pressure-tested with nitrogen and checked for leaks before evacuation and charging.
  • Precision refrigerant charging — the final refrigerant charge is verified using the manufacturer's specified operating targets, including superheat and/or subcooling, rather than relying solely on the amount weighed into the system.

Electrical & Mechanical Installation

  • Code-compliant electrical connection — equipment is connected using the properly sized breaker and wiring, an appropriate service disconnect, and proper bonding and grounding.
  • Lineset insulation and protection — the refrigerant lineset is fully insulated and securely supported along its entire run, with UV-rated protection wherever the insulation is exposed to outdoor conditions.
  • Condensate drainage — condensate piping is properly sloped, tested for drainage, and installed with consideration for winter conditions and potential freeze-up.
  • Outdoor unit installation — the outdoor unit is installed level and positioned with the required clearances for proper airflow, service access, and winter/snow conditions.

System Commissioning

  • Complete system commissioning — the finished installation is fully tested to verify proper operation, including airflow, heating and cooling operation, system controls, and overall performance.
  • Wi-Fi controller setup — the smart controller is configured, connected to Wi-Fi, paired with the equipment, and tested to ensure the controls are functioning properly.
  • Final functional testing — the complete system is operated and checked before the installation is considered complete.

Removal of Existing Equipment

  • Old equipment removal — existing equipment is removed as required, with refrigerant properly recovered and handled in accordance with applicable environmental requirements.

Specific to your install

Existing Components Reused

  • Existing 40A breaker and circuit wiring — the existing electrical circuit will be reused for the new equipment.
  • Existing copper lineset — the existing refrigerant lines will be thoroughly flushed and pressure-tested as part of the installation to ensure they are suitable for reuse.
  • Existing oil furnace — the existing furnace will remain in place and continue to operate as the backup heat source, allowing the system to function as a hybrid/dual-fuel setup.

New Components Included

  • High-efficiency indoor coil — new coil matched to the new heat pump system.
  • Custom sheet-metal fabrication — custom fabrication at the furnace/plenum to properly connect and transition the new indoor coil to the existing ductwork.
  • Wi-Fi dual-fuel smart controller — allows the heat pump and oil furnace to work together automatically, with intelligent control over when the system switches between heating sources.
  • Wall bracket/stand — new mounting support for the outdoor unit.
  • Outdoor unit relocation — the heat pump will be relocated to a new position so it does not obstruct the existing window.
